Specification and gaming-resistance of a cross-lab safety test harness

Research question

What exactly does a shared cross-lab harness measure, and how do you keep participants from optimizing against the harness instead of the underlying safety property?

Why

If the peer-review gate becomes the release criterion, the harness becomes the target; without held-out tasks and rotating authorship it degrades into another benchmark the producer controls. This determines whether cross-lab review is a real trust-boundary shift or theater.

Detecting and attributing autonomous agents that probe published models

Research question

Can agentic probing of model endpoints be distinguished from legitimate human traffic by request signature, and what rate/anomaly controls reduce blast radius without breaking normal use?

Why

Open-weight and open-endpoint publication stops being a one-time distribution event and becomes an ongoing, instrumented attack surface. Operators need detection and attribution before they can constrain anything.

Source video ↗

A software substitute for 'physics as judge' in acceptance testing

Research question

When a domain has no physical oracle, do externally imposed executable acceptance tests (replay a real task, run the code, hit an environment) correlate better with real-world failure than internally authored rubrics?

Why

The credibility of an evaluation comes from the criterion being immune to argument. Without a physics-equivalent, agent and model evaluation defaults back to a rubric the builder writes, which is exactly the failure mode the physics analogy warns against.

Source video ↗

Orchestrator minimalism: coordination layers as cost, not value

Research question

Does collapsing intermediate orchestration tiers and provisioning context, tools, and permissions directly to the worker improve per-step latency and failure attribution relative to multi-hop orchestration?

Why

If management's value is friction removal rather than coordination, then agent architectures built around routing and reporting tiers are paying a per-step tax for central control they may not need. The measurement is latency, context overhead, and how cleanly failures localize.

Source video ↗

Reusability economics applied to expensive computation artifacts

Research question

For long-horizon agent workloads, does reusing cached context, plans, or warm state beat recomputing per run, and where is the crossover point?

Why

The reusability argument is the dominant cost lever in launch economics because build cost is high and reuse cycles are cheap. If the same structure holds for agent state, per-run recomputation is the wrong optimization target.

Your Release Gate Should Not Live Inside Your Organization

Self-certified safety evaluation is structurally weak because the producer controls the acceptance criterion; credible pre-release safety requires a portable, externally executed harness whose result is issued by a party with different incentives.

Angle

Contrast internal red-teaming with an inter-organization harness, then specify the interface contract such a harness needs: access model, harness spec, and result format.

Source video ↗

Open Weights Are a Live Endpoint, Not a File

Publishing model weights is not a one-time distribution event; it creates continuous, unsolicited runtime traffic from autonomous clients, which makes telemetry, rate limiting, and attribution first-class components of the distribution pipeline rather than afterthoughts.

Angle

Treat publication as a deployment: enumerate the monitoring and abuse-detection surface that must ship alongside weights.

Source video ↗

Nobody Argues With Physics: Why Executable Oracles Beat Authored Rubrics

Evaluation is only trustworthy when the acceptance criterion cannot be negotiated by the team being evaluated; executable, externally imposed tests beat rubrics the builder writes, and the hard engineering problem is constructing a software equivalent of a physical oracle.

Angle

Use hardware verification as the reference model, then test how far replayable task execution and real-environment checks can carry evaluation in domains without a physical ground truth.

Project ideas

4

Cross-Lab Harness Exchange

gatehouse

Externally authored, held-out tasks will surface safety-relevant failure modes that the model owner's own evaluation suite misses at a substantial and stable rate.

Proof of concept

Build a harness runner with a fixed task interface and result schema, let participants author tasks in rotation, and run one org's suite against an artifact produced by a different org.

Measurement

Fraction of externally found failures absent from the owner's own results, plus score drift of previously passing artifacts when task authorship rotates.

Source video ↗

Probe Sentinel

movement-lab

Autonomous agents that systematically elicit capabilities from a published model produce request signatures (rapid prompt sweeps, systematic capability enumeration) that separate from human traffic at usable precision and recall.

Proof of concept

Instrument a model-serving endpoint with per-session features and evaluate detection against labeled agentic probing traffic versus normal usage, with rate controls attached.

Measurement

Detection precision/recall on probe traffic and the false-positive rate imposed on legitimate users by the resulting rate controls.

Source video ↗

Oracle Acceptance Bench

beyond-evals

Replayable, externally imposed executable acceptance tests correlate with downstream real-world failure better than internally authored rubric scores on the same agent outputs.

Proof of concept

Take a long-horizon agent task set, score it both by rubric and by executing the produced artifact in the target environment, then compare rankings against observed production failures.

Measurement

Rank correlation between each scoring method and independently observed task failure, plus disagreement rate between rubric and execution on the same run.

Source video ↗

Artifact Reuse Amortizer

movement-lab

Above a threshold of context and plan build cost, reusing warm state across runs reduces total cost and latency without degrading reliability compared to recomputing from scratch.

Proof of concept

Run the same long-horizon agent workload in two modes, cached/reused artifact versus full recompute, and sweep build cost and reuse-cycle count to locate the crossover.

Measurement

Cost and latency per run and reliability delta (task success rate) as a function of reuse count and artifact build cost.

Architectural implications

3

Safety validation is proposed as an inter-organization harness rather than an internal step.

Before

Model owner runs its own red-team and evaluation suite, then releases.

After

Competing labs execute a shared harness against each other's models before release, producing externally issued safety evidence.

Consequence

Evaluation becomes a portable, org-agnostic artifact with an interface contract (access, harness spec, result format), and the release gate moves outside the producer's boundary.

Source video ↗

Autonomous agents probing open-source models implies ongoing, unsolicited runtime interaction with published artifacts.

Before

Publishing weights is treated as a one-time distribution event.

After

Published models are treated as live, continuously probed endpoints requiring traffic observation and abuse detection.

Consequence

Model distribution pipelines must incorporate telemetry, rate limiting, and attribution as first-class components, not afterthoughts.

Source video ↗

Management defined as friction removal suggests the orchestration layer's job is to shorten the executor's path to the problem.

Before

Orchestrators add planning, routing, and reporting tiers between the goal and the worker.

After

Orchestrators primarily provision context, tools, and permissions directly to the worker and step out of the loop.

Consequence

Fewer intermediate hops, lower per-step latency and context overhead, and simpler failure attribution — at the cost of less central control and observability.
