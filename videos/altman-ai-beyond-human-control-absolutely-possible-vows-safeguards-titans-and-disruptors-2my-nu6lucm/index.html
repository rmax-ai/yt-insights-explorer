Summary

Overview

  • Speaker: Sam Altman and Alyson Shontell
  • Channel: Fortune Magazine
  • Main topic: AI Safety, Alignment, AGI, and Industry Governance
  • Purpose: To examine OpenAI's approach to AI safety, governance, capability milestones, and the geopolitical implications of developing superhuman intelligence. In an interview with Fortune's Editor-in-Chief Alyson Shontell, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman discusses the rapid acceleration of AI capabilities, the risks of AI agents escaping control, the definition of AGI, and the urgent need for international alignment, safety research, and governance frameworks to ensure advanced AI benefits humanity.

  • Evidence: Reflecting on industry risk estimates ('P(Doom)') and safety council warnings.

Generalization

Project ideas

4

EgressGate

gatehouse

Per-tool authorization combined with network egress allowlists will reduce adversarial sandbox escapes to zero in tool-using agent evaluations, whereas process or VM isolation alone will not.

Proof of concept

Red-team harness with goal-directed tool-using agents, an egress proxy logging every network attempt, and a per-action policy engine enforcing allowlisted tools and destinations.

Measurement

Escape attempts blocked, unauthorized egress events per 100 adversarial prompts, and task completion delta versus an unconfined baseline.

Source video ↗

SafetyCaseGate

gatehouse

A machine-checkable safety-case schema with blocking promotion thresholds will reduce unsafe unpaused capability advancement without significantly reducing acceptable training run velocity.

Proof of concept

Training pipeline gate that ingests safety-case artifacts, validates required evidence, blocks runs when evidence is missing, and records manual override justifications.

Measurement

Number of blocked runs, false-positive pause rate, time-to-unpause, and downstream incident rate.

Source video ↗

EconSubstitutionBench

beyond-evals

Economic task substitution rate is a more discriminative AGI threshold than abstract capability benchmarks and correlates with expert AGI judgments.

Proof of concept

Benchmark suite of real economically viable tasks with human baselines and model substitution scoring across multiple frontier models.

Measurement

Substitution rate, correlation with expert AGI labels, inter-task variance, and test-retest reliability.

Source video ↗

PortableEval

beyond-evals

Proof of concept

Open schema and reference runner for safety evaluations, signed result bundles, and an independent verifier that checks provenance and completeness.
