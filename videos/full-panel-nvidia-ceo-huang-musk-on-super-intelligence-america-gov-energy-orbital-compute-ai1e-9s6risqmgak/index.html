Safety containment and monitoring are being implemented with dedicated hardware and isolated runtimes, not just software policies.

Before

Agent safety relied on software sandboxing, logging, and after-the-fact audits.

After

Agents run in contained environments (e.g., browsers) with hardware-accelerated real-time behavior monitoring (Bluefield) and automated escalation (Open Shell).

Consequence

Safety becomes a first-class architectural concern, potentially adding overhead but enabling finer-grained control over agent actions.

After

Consequence

Latency, data transfer costs, and fault tolerance must be redesigned for geographically distributed or remote execution.
