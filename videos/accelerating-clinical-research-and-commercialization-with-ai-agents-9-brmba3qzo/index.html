Architectural implications

3

Safety moved from an afterthought to a mandatory layer before orchestration.

Before

Safety checks often applied after generation or sporadically.

After

A dedicated guardrails layer intercepts all user queries before any agent processing.

Consequence

Higher compliance and trust, but potential added latency and a single point of failure if guardrails are too strict or malfunction.

Source video ↗

Data access logic is encapsulated in dedicated data agents, rather than being embedded in orchestrators.

Before

Orchestrator agents directly call APIs or query databases.

After

Orchestrators delegate to specialized data agents that know how to retrieve and process specific data types.

Consequence

Improved modularity and reuse, but increased coordination overhead and need for robust inter-agent communication.

Source video ↗

Use of specialized models (retrieval, reasoning, customization) instead of a single model.

Before

One large language model handles all tasks (retrieval, reasoning, generation).

After

Multiple specialized models: Nemotron for reasoning, NeMo Retriever for retrieval, NeMo Customizer for tuning.

Consequence

Better performance on domain-specific tasks, but requires managing multiple models, versions, and their integration.
